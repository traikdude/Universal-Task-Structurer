# From loose notes to a checklist

Universal Task Structurer helps people turn material from notes, whiteboards and documents into reviewable tasks. The illustration follows the original sketchnote style and traces that single action from left to right.

**Primary prompt** — Wide 2:1 editorial cut-paper sketchnote illustration for Universal Task Structurer. Overhead on warm ivory drafting paper: a loose spiral notebook with abstract pencil scribbles, a little whiteboard sketch and folded document on the left. Three hand-inked flowing strands converge at a gold binder clip then fan into neatly aligned deep teal task index cards on the right with empty checkboxes and indented subtask lines. Sage green, ink blue, ochre, coral pencil, tactile paper shadows, generous margins. No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI chrome, or devices.
